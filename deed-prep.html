Quit Claim Deed Preparation

Benjamin Legacy Group offers Quit Claim Deed preparation services for real estate investors and property sellers who need a properly prepared conveyance document.

What You Provide

  • Buyer and seller (grantor/grantee) information
  • Legal description and parcel or property details
  • Prior deed or title reference
  • Marital status and vesting instructions
Sample intake list — update to match your exact process.

What We Prepare

  • Quit Claim Deed prepared using the property, ownership, and vesting information provided by the client
  • Formatting prepared for county recording
  • Review for accuracy against the information provided
  • Delivery for signature and notarization
