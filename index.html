Meet the Founder

Trina Davis

Trina began investing in tax deed real estate in December 2024 while undergoing dialysis. What began as an effort to create an additional source of income developed into an active real estate investment business.

She continued researching markets, analyzing properties, participating in tax sales, and completing real-world transactions while preparing for a kidney transplant.

After receiving a kidney transplant in September 2025, she continued building Benjamin Legacy Group around the same principle that shaped her investing journey: Building Legacy Through Real Estate.
